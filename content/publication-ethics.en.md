## Publication Ethics Statement

The Proceedings of the 27th IMEKO TC4 Conference 2026 with Special TC11 Technical Session are published by IMEKO – International Measurement Confederation. The Editor, the Organizing Committee and the International Program Committee are committed to upholding the integrity of the scientific record and to preventing publication malpractice. This statement follows the Core Practices of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and is binding for all authors, reviewers and editors involved in the Proceedings.

### Duties of Authors

**Originality and plagiarism.** Authors must submit only original work. Where the work or words of others are used, they must be appropriately cited or quoted. Plagiarism in any form, including self-plagiarism and the reuse of substantial parts of the authors' own previously published work without attribution, is unacceptable. Originality is assessed by the reviewers and the Editor, and submissions may be screened with plagiarism-detection software. Manuscripts in which plagiarism is detected are rejected or removed from the Proceedings.

**Multiple, redundant or concurrent submission.** Manuscripts must not have been published previously and must not be under consideration by another conference or journal. Submitting the same manuscript to more than one venue at the same time is unethical. Extended versions of conference papers submitted to journal special issues must cite the conference paper and must comply with the originality requirements of the journal.

**Authorship.** Authorship is limited to those who have made a significant contribution to the conception, design, execution or interpretation of the reported work. All persons who made such contributions must be listed as co-authors. The corresponding author ensures that all co-authors have approved the final version of the manuscript and agreed to its submission. Artificial intelligence tools do not qualify for authorship.

**Acknowledgement of sources and funding.** Publications that have influenced the reported work must be cited. Sources of financial support for the research must be disclosed.

**Conflicts of interest.** Authors must disclose any financial or other substantive conflict of interest that could be construed to influence the results or their interpretation.

**Accuracy of data.** Authors must present an accurate account of the work performed and an objective discussion of its significance. Fabrication, falsification and selective reporting of data are unacceptable. Authors should be prepared to provide the underlying data on reasonable request.

**Use of generative AI tools.** Generative AI tools may be used only to improve the language and readability of a manuscript, not to create or alter scientific content, data, figures or results. Any such use must be disclosed in the manuscript. Authors remain fully responsible for the content of their work.

**Errors in published work.** When authors discover a significant error or inaccuracy in their published paper, they must promptly notify the Editor and cooperate in correcting or retracting the paper.

### Duties of Reviewers

**Contribution to editorial decisions.** Peer review assists the Editor and the Technical Programme Co-Chairs in making editorial decisions and helps authors improve their manuscripts. Each full paper is evaluated by at least two independent reviewers. Reviewers who feel unqualified to review a manuscript, or who cannot review it within the given time, should notify the Editor and withdraw from the review.

**Confidentiality.** Manuscripts received for review are confidential documents. They must not be shown to or discussed with others, and must not be uploaded to generative AI tools or other external services.

**Objectivity.** Reviews are conducted objectively, with clearly formulated and supported arguments. Personal criticism of the authors is inappropriate.

**Conflicts of interest.** Reviewers must not review manuscripts in which they have a conflict of interest resulting from competitive, collaborative or other relationships with any of the authors, companies or institutions connected to the paper. Reviewers do not review papers of which they are authors or co-authors. Unpublished material disclosed in a manuscript must not be used in a reviewer's own research without the express written consent of the authors.

**Acknowledgement of sources.** Reviewers should identify relevant published work that has not been cited by the authors and should alert the Editor to any substantial similarity or overlap between the manuscript and other published work of which they are aware.

### Duties of the Editor and the Committees

**Publication decisions.** The Editor, the General Conference Chair and the Technical Programme Co-Chairs are responsible for deciding which of the submitted papers are published. Decisions are based on the reviewers' reports, the scientific merit and originality of the work and its relevance to the scope of IMEKO TC4 and TC11, and are not influenced by commercial considerations.

**Fair play.** Manuscripts are evaluated solely on their intellectual content, without regard to the race, gender, sexual orientation, religious belief, ethnic origin, citizenship, institutional affiliation or political philosophy of the authors.

**Confidentiality.** Information about a submitted manuscript is not disclosed to anyone other than the corresponding author, the reviewers, potential reviewers and the publisher, as appropriate.

**Conflicts of interest.** Members of the editorial team recuse themselves from handling manuscripts in which they have a conflict of interest, including papers of which they are authors or co-authors. Unpublished material disclosed in a submitted manuscript is not used in the editors' own research without the express written consent of the authors.

### Corrections, Retractions and Misconduct

Allegations of misconduct, whether raised before or after publication, are investigated by the Editor in cooperation with the Organizing Committee and, where appropriate, the authors' institutions, following the COPE guidelines. Authors are given the opportunity to respond. If misconduct is confirmed, the manuscript is rejected or, if already published, retracted from the Proceedings and a retraction notice is published. Corrections of genuine errors that do not invalidate the results are published as errata. Complaints and appeals concerning the review process or the published content should be addressed to the Editor at [imeko2026@fel.cvut.cz](mailto:imeko2026@fel.cvut.cz).
