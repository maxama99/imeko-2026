The Organizing Committee would like to sincerely thank all authors, speakers, session chairs, keynote lecturers, and participants who made the 27th IMEKO TC4 Conference 2026 with Special TC11 Technical Session such a success. Held on 16–18 September 2026 at the Czech Technical University in Prague under the motto _"Measuring the Future"_, the conference brought together the international measurement community for three days of high-quality presentations, inspiring keynotes, and valuable discussions. We are grateful for your contributions and for the friendly, collaborative atmosphere throughout the event.

- **Best Paper Presented by a Young Scientist** — Changhao Zhao (National Institute of Metrology, China), for the paper _"High-Temperature Superconductor Josephson Junction Arrays Coupled to Microwave of Different Polarizations."_
